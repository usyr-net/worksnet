Refer & earn

Invite friends.
Earn balance.

Earn balance credit every time someone you invite makes their first purchase.

How It Works

1.  Open the bot, tap My Account, then tap Refer to get your personal invite link.

2.  Share your link with friends.

3.  When someone joins through your link and makes their first purchase, $0.50 is added to your balance.

Rewards

You earn $0.50 in balance credit for every person you invite who completes a first purchase. Any purchase counts, and there is no minimum amount.

Rewards are added to your balance automatically shortly after your friend's first purchase.

Using Your Rewards

Referral rewards are added to your WrksNet balance and can be used on any service in the bot. Like all account balance, referral credit cannot be withdrawn as cash.

Rules & Warnings

Please share your invite link responsibly. Do not spam, and do not post your link anywhere it is not allowed. Abuse of the referral program can result in rewards being withheld or your account being restricted.
